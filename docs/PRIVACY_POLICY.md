# Privacy Policy for E-Sign: Sign PDF & Documents

**Last Updated:** November 4, 2026

Thank you for choosing **E-Sign: Sign PDF & Documents** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by **Ranbir Saini**. Your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to transparency, zero data collection, and local on-device security.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Privacy by Design
E-Sign operates with a **zero-knowledge, offline-first** architecture:
- **No Document Uploads:** All PDF files, signatures, annotations, and edited images remain strictly on your physical device. We do not have servers, databases, or cloud storage that store your documents.
- **No Account Mandatory:** You can sign documents, edit PDFs, and compress images completely offline without creating an account.

---

## 2. Secure Vault & AES-256 Encryption
- Any file or media locked inside the **Secure Vault** is encrypted locally using industry-standard **AES-256-GCM** encryption.
- Encryption keys and master passwords are kept only in your device's hardware-backed secure storage (such as Android Keystore / Flutter Secure Storage).
- We have no backdoor, master recovery key, or remote access to your vaulted content. If you forget your password, the encrypted files cannot be decrypted by anyone, including the developer.

---

## 3. Device Permissions
E-Sign may request certain device permissions solely to provide core application features:
- **Storage / Photos / Media:** Required to import documents, PDFs, or photos for signing, editing, or vaulting, and to export completed documents back to your gallery or downloads folder.
- **Camera (Optional):** Used only if you choose to scan physical paper documents or capture live photos to sign.
- **Internet / Network State (Optional):** Used for non-sensitive diagnostics (crash reporting via Firebase Crashlytics) and displaying non-intrusive advertisements (Google AdMob SDK) where enabled. No personal document data is ever transmitted.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
The app may utilize trusted third-party SDKs strictly for operational reliability and analytics:
- **Google Play Services**
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**
- **Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics**

These services may gather anonymous device metrics (e.g. app crash logs, device model, operating system version) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
E-Sign does not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may periodically update this policy to reflect new capabilities or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published to this repository and live website.
