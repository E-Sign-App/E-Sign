Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Are my signed documents and passwords stored on your servers?
No! E-Sign is strictly Zero-Knowledge. In Guest mode, all PDFs, images, and signatures remain 100% on your local device. Encrypted files in the Secure Vault use a password that is never transmitted or saved anywhere. If you forget your password, even we cannot decrypt your file.
How does the shared .esec vault file open on another phone?
When you share an encrypted file via WhatsApp or email, the recipient receives a protected .esec file and a download link. When tapped on their Android device, E-Sign opens automatically, prompts for the password, verifies the cryptographic authentication tag, and smoothly launches the built-in media viewer.
Can I use E-Sign without creating an account?
Yes! E-Sign features full offline Guest Mode. You can sign unlimited documents, convert formats, optimize images, and lock vault files completely offline without an internet connection or account sign-up.
Are electronic signatures created with E-Sign legally valid?
Yes. Digital electronic signatures made in E-Sign comply with electronic signature acts (such as the US ESIGN Act and European eIDAS) for standard agreements, invoices, NDA forms, and rental contracts.
